Professional practice

Focus areas of my teaching practicum.

  • Student-centred lessons. Planning and delivering learner-focused English units under mentor-teacher supervision.
  • Digital materials. Interactive activities and Google Classroom materials designed around classroom needs.
  • CLIL / TPACK lesson design. Combining content, pedagogy and technology in one lesson plan.
  • Authentic assessment. Using speaking tasks and activity results to assess what students can do in practice.
  • Reflective practice. Documenting evidence and good practice in my E-Teaching Portfolio and reflecting on it with mentors and peers.
  • Parents and community. Communicating with families and respecting local culture.
