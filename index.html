Professional practice

What I work on as part of my teaching-profession practicum.

  • Student-centred lessons. Planning and delivering learner-focused English units under mentor-teacher supervision.
  • Digital materials. Interactive games and Google Classroom activities designed for real classroom needs.
  • CLIL / TPACK lesson design. Combining content, pedagogy and technology in one lesson plan.
  • Authentic assessment. Using game results and speaking tasks to see what children can really do.
  • Reflective practice. Collecting evidence and best practice in my E-Teaching Portfolio and sharing it in a professional learning community (PLC).
  • Parents and community. Working with families and respecting local culture and wisdom.
